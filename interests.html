WA State Race Time Predictor

Enter your race times from courses given in the drop box to predict your race time at the WA state XC meet. The given uncertainty represents the standard deviation in the prediction. Using multiple races should produce lower uncertainty. Inputting one race at a time also allows you to compare results across the different courses (a faster predicted state time implies a better race).
